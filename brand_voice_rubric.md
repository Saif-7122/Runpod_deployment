# Brand Voice & Ad Quality Rubric

This rubric guides both LLM-assisted evaluation and programmatic quality checks for all generated short-form ads.

## Core Scoring Pillars (Total: 100 Points)

### 1. Hook Velocity & Attention (Weight: 25 Points)
- **Immediate Visual/Text Hook**: The opening 0–2.5 seconds must feature high-contrast, provocative copy (e.g., questioning urban stress, pollution, or lost freedom).
- **No Slow Build-Ups**: Avoid logo animations or passive landscape shots without punchy overlay copy in Scene 1.
- **Pass Threshold**: Hook text must clearly define the stakes within 2.5 seconds.

### 2. Pacing, Dynamics & Visual Variety (Weight: 25 Points)
- **Rapid Scene Pacing**: Every single scene cut MUST be $\le 3.5\text{ seconds}$. Any cut exceeding 3.5 seconds receives an immediate 15-point penalty.
- **Visual Contrast**: B-roll must avoid repetitive camera angles (mix drone aerials, lush closeups, lifestyle, and infrastructure).
- **Text Overlay Alignment**: Overlays must reinforce, not duplicate verbatim, the voiceover script.
- **Shot-to-Line Match**: The footage under every line must visibly show what the line says (a clubhouse line over a clubhouse shot). Mismatched footage loses up to 10 points.

### 3. Offer Clarity & Value Proposition (Weight: 25 Points)
- **Clear Value Demonstration**: Highlights tangible benefits (proximity, gated security, clear title deeds, lifestyle elevation).
- **Customer Relatability**: Speaks directly to the target buyer persona (nature retreat, generational wealth, clean breathing space).
- **Brevity**: Sentences must be concise, spoken rhythmically without dense jargon.

### 4. Brand Voice, Compliance & Call-to-Action (Weight: 25 Points)
- **Tone Profile**: Confident, authentic, aspirational, and grounded. Premium feel without tacky urgency.
- **Zero Tolerance for Banned Financial Claims**:
  - Strictly forbidden terms: *"100% guaranteed", "risk-free", "instant riches", "zero risk", "guaranteed returns", "double your money"*.
  - Any detected violation results in an immediate 50-point deduction and automatic disqualification.
- **Decisive Call to Action (CTA)**: The final 3 seconds must provide a single, friction-free action (e.g., *"Tap below to book your private weekend site tour."*).

---

## Decision Thresholds
- **Score $\ge 80$**: **APPROVED** for TTS synthesis, video rendering, and client upload.
- **Score $< 80$**: **REVISE** (Loops back to Director with detailed feedback notes; capped at 3 retries).
